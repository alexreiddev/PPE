Problems we solve

Start with the hazard. We match the kit and prove it with the standard.

These are the problems site managers and HSE leads bring us most. Each one has a documented line behind it, with the marking your method statement asks for.

Hand injuries on the rig floor

Impact and pinch points during rigging and pipe handling cause most hand injuries. The R-267 puts TPR guards over every knuckle and finger, with a TefLoc palm that keeps its grip in oil.

ANSI/ISEA 138 L2  /  EN 388 4243BP See the impact glove

Electrical and puncture hazards underfoot

Live cables, nails and chemical spills on the same site. The Petroking 3228 and 3229 are EH rated with a puncture-resistant insole, a non-metallic toe and ForceGuard chemical resistance. Made in Italy, EU 35–48.

EN ISO 20345  /  ASTM F2413  /  EH  /  PR See the Petroking boots

Heavy boots in the UAE heat

Crews who walk all shift stop wearing boots that are too heavy and too hot. The 6338 is a breathable nylon safety trainer with an aluminium toe — protection they will actually keep on.

ASTM F2413-24  /  I/C  /  EH  /  SR See the athletic shoe

No phones allowed in the Ex zone

An ordinary phone is an ignition source, so crews in hazardous areas lose contact. The Smart-Ex 03 is certified for Zone 1/21, with push-to-talk, an alarm button and lone worker protection.

ATEX Zone 1/21  /  Div 1 See the Ex smartphone

About us

Every worker returns home safely.

That is the whole point of what we sell, and the standard we hold ourselves to.

Oasis Star General Trading was established in the UAE to supply the construction sector with safety equipment it can rely on. We work with leading manufacturers to bring in personal protective equipment that is durable, tested and certified to international standards — and priced so that meeting regulations is not a burden.

Our customers are site managers, HSE leads and procurement teams who need the right kit, in the right sizes, when the programme says so. We help them meet their obligations and build a culture where protection is expected rather than enforced.
