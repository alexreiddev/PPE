About us

Every worker returns home safely.

That is the whole point of what we sell, and the standard we hold ourselves to.

Oasis Star General Trading was established in the UAE to supply the construction sector with safety equipment it can rely on. We work with leading manufacturers to bring in personal protective equipment that is durable, tested and certified to international standards — and priced so that meeting regulations is not a burden.

Our customers are site managers, HSE leads and procurement teams who need the right kit, in the right sizes, when the programme says so. We help them meet their obligations and build a culture where protection is expected rather than enforced.
