About Oasis Star

Every worker returns home safely.

Oasis Star General Trading supplies certified personal protective equipment to the UAE’s construction sector — head to toe, tested to international standards.

Who we are

Committed to protecting lives.

We were established in the UAE to give construction sites safety equipment they can rely on, and we have built our reputation as a dependable partner in workplace safety.

Our mission is to make sure every worker returns home safely, by equipping sites with durable, tested and certified gear. We partner with leading manufacturers to bring in products that combine comfort, performance and affordability — so meeting the regulations is not a burden on the programme.

We help businesses meet their safety obligations while building a culture of protection and preparedness on site.

How we work

Built around how sites actually buy.

01

Head to toe

Safety helmets, gloves, coveralls, safety footwear, reflective vests, eye and face protection, and Zone 1/21 certified devices — one supplier for the whole kit list.

Certified, and documented
